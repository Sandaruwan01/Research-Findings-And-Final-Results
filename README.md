## Research Objectives
The primary objective of this research was to design, implement, and evaluate a game-based learning platform, **“Sorting Arcade,”** to address the persistent challenges students face when learning sorting algorithms. 

The study successfully demonstrated that gamification, when combined with interactive visualization and usability-driven design, can significantly enhance learners’ comprehension and engagement. Through pre- and post-assessments, learners showed notable improvement in understanding key sorting algorithms:
* Bubble Sort
* Insertion Sort
* Selection Sort
* Merge Sort
* Quick Sort

The system also incorporated multiple-choice questions (MCQs) before and after gameplay to triangulated and validate learning outcomes.

---

##  Methodology
The evaluation followed a **pre-test/post-test design** with a single group of participants (no separate control group was employed). The focus was on measuring direct learning gains within the same group after exposure to the game-based learning environment.

The average pre-test and post-test scores across all participants were compared to determine the improvement attributable to the platform, expressed both in absolute score increase and percentage gain.

---

##  Results & Key Findings
The analysis was conducted on data collected from **20 students**. The results revealed a substantial improvement in students’ understanding of sorting algorithms after engaging with the **Sorting Arcade**

| Metric |  Percentage |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Group Initial Average (Pre-Test)** | 64.40% |
| **Group Final Average (Post-Test)** | 80.70% |
| **Overall Group Improvement** | **+16.30%** |
